Delete Your GuoTalk Account

Developer: GuoTalk  ·  App: GuoTalk: Speak Mandarin (com.guotalk.app)

This page explains how to delete your GuoTalk account and the personal data associated with it. You can delete your account directly inside the app, or request deletion by email if you no longer have access to the app.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

Deleting from within the app removes your account immediately.

  1. Open the GuoTalk app.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

Email: guotalk2026@gmail.com

Please use the subject line "Delete my GuoTalk account" and send it from the email address associated with your account so we can verify the request. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your account record (email address and hashed password).
  • Your learning progress — lessons completed, vocabulary, streaks, review history, and mistakes.
  • Your subscription status record held in our system.
  • Any usage records tied to your account identifier.

What is not stored or is handled separately

  • Voice recordings are never stored — they are processed for pronunciation feedback and discarded immediately, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Your subscription/billing is managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — please cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings to stop future charges.
  • Guest data (if you used the app without an account) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Data retention

Account data is retained only while your account is active. Once you request deletion, the data listed above is removed within 30 days and is not recoverable afterward. We may retain a minimal record of the deletion request itself where required to comply with legal obligations.
